Selected case studies
Global Release Governance Platform
Light & Wonder
Problem
10+ active code branches across customer environments with no consistent release visibility. Deployment risk was high and production readiness reviews were ad hoc, leading to inconsistent outcomes across global properties.
Action
Designed and implemented a release tracking and governance process covering branch management, stakeholder communication, environment readiness criteria, and structured go/no-go checkpoints before each customer deployment.
Result
Improved release traceability across all customer environments, reduced deployment risk, and established a repeatable production readiness standard that could scale with the portfolio of global properties.
Enterprise Support Transformation
Light & Wonder
Problem
Ticket response times were inconsistent and SLA accountability was unclear across a globally distributed support function. Escalation paths were informal and resolution metrics were not visible to leadership.
Action
Built SLA governance framework, deployed ServiceNow dashboards for real-time visibility, formalized escalation workflows, and established ownership accountability at the manager level across regions.
Result
Achieved an 80% improvement in response and resolution times within 60 days. Leadership gained live operational visibility and managers had clear accountability structures for the first time.
Greenfield Resort Technology Buildout
Hard Rock Sacramento

A 300,000 sq ft gaming and hospitality property needed full technology infrastructure built from ground zero — including network, servers, storage, endpoints, AV, and all production systems — in a compressed timeline before opening.
Action

Result
Delivered the full technology buildout in six months, on time and within scope. The execution model became the franchise template for subsequent Hard Rock property openings, extending the impact across the brand.
